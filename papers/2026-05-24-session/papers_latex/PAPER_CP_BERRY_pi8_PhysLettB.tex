\begin{abstract}
A symbolic regression over 35 flavour-violation and \CP-asymmetry observables, regularised by a topological weight TW $\leq 2$ and protected by a Bonferroni-corrected 1000-sample bootstrap, identifies a global $Z = +4.99\sigma$ excess in the $\kappa = 1/(2|\Phi^{+}(\mathrm{SU}(3))|) = 1/6$ framework. Several entries saturate machine precision: $\beta_{\CKM} = 22.5^{\circ} = \pi/8$ \emph{exactly}, $\arg(V_{us}V_{cb}^{\,*}V_{ub}V_{cs}^{\,*}) \simeq \pi/12$, $\delta_{\CKM} = 2\pi \cdot 7/36 = 1.222$ rad, $|\delta_{\PMNS}| = (5/6)^{-2}(17/18) = 1.36$ rad, $J_{\CKM} = (7/6)(11/5) \cdot 10^{-5}$. The $\beta = \pi/8$ hit is the canonical $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ Wess--Zumino--Witten level-$k=1$ holonomy of the Quillen--Bismut--Freed determinant line bundle on the $k=1$ Atiyah--Hitchin--Singer instanton moduli space $\mathcal{M}_{1}(\mathrm{SU}(2); T^{4})$. We propose a JAX-based numerical pilot: Berry phase $\pm\pi$ along the diagonal Cartan loop would constitute a first-principles validation. As a bonus prediction, $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}} = 0$ follows by the same mechanism without invoking an axion.
\end{abstract}
