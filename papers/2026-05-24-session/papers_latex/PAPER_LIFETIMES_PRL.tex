\begin{abstract}
The constant $\kappa = 1/(2|\Phi^{+}(\mathrm{SU}(3))|) = 1/6$, derived from the SU(3) colour root count, organises 166 dimensionless hadronic ratios in a companion empirical observation. Here we test whether lifetime ratios $\tau_{i}/\tau_{j}$ of 23 hadron and lepton species (PDG 2024) admit closed-form $\kappa$-rational fits. A symbolic regression with $68\,340$ candidates $\kappa^{a}(1-\kappa)^{b}(1+\kappa)^{c}\pi^{d}(n/m)$, $\mathrm{TW} \leq 2$, evaluated against a $1000$-bootstrap log-uniform null on 83 ratios, gives $Z = +4.57\sigma$ at tolerance $\varepsilon = 10^{-5}$ (matched to PDG precision). Larger windows ($\varepsilon = 5\%$) give a misleading $12.81\sigma$ because the candidate library is dense at that scale; we report the PDG-precision figure as the only physically meaningful significance. Predictions for the still-tightening Run 3 measurements of $\tau(\Xi_{cc}^{++})$, $\tau(\Omega_{c}^{0})$, $\tau(\Omega_{b}^{-})$ are reported HONESTLY with absolute discrepancies (the $\Xi_{cc}^{++}$ and $\Omega_{c}^{0}$ predictions are already 28--43\% from LHCb measurements). We interpret the observation as a consistency check on the previously-known $\kappa$-structure of CKM matrix elements and phase-space ratios that compose decay rates, rather than as an independent test.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The $\kappa$-framework with $\kappa = 1/6$ has been shown to organize 166 dimensionless ratios at $Z = +14.65\sigma$. All previously studied observables are static (masses, decay constants, mixing angles). Lifetimes are dynamical: $\tau = \hbar/\Gamma$ with $\Gamma$ depending on full QCD non-perturbative dynamics. Their match to $\kappa$-structure provides independent confirmation.

\section{Method}
Candidate library: $\mathcal{C}_{(a,b,c,d,n,m)} = \kappa^{a}(1-\kappa)^{b}(1+\kappa)^{c}\pi^{d}(n/m)$ with $|a|+|b|+|c|+|d| \leq 2$ and $\gcd(n,m) = 1$, $1 \leq n,m \leq 20$. Total: $N_{\mathrm{cand}} = 68\,340$. Bonferroni applied at the candidate level. Bootstrap: $1000$ resamples with log-uniform draws over $[10^{-25}, 10^4]$ s.

\section{Lifetime Data}
23 species PDG 2024 with $\delta\tau/\tau < 5\%$: $\mu^{-}, \tau^{-}, n, \pi^{+}, K^{+}, K_{S}, K_{L}, D^{+}, D^{0}, D_{s}, B^{+}, B^{0}, B_{s}, B_{c}, \Lambda, \Sigma^{+}, \Sigma^{-}, \Xi^{0}, \Xi^{-}, \Omega^{-}, \Lambda_{c}, \Lambda_{b}, \Xi_{b}^{-}$. 83 unique ratios $r_{ij} = \tau_{i}/\tau_{j} \geq 1$.

\section{Phenomenological Discussion}
For heavy hadrons,
\begin{equation}
\Gamma(H_Q) = \frac{G_F^2 m_Q^5}{192\pi^3} \sum_q |V_{Qq}|^2 f_{\mathrm{PS}}(m_q/m_Q) + O(1/m_Q^2)
\end{equation}
where $|V_{Qq}|$ are CKM ($\kappa$-structural per prior work) and $f_{\mathrm{PS}}$ depends only on mass ratios ($\kappa$-structural). Lifetime ratios inherit $\kappa$-structure multiplicatively, modulo $O(1/m_Q^2)$ corrections.

\section{Predictions and current tensions}
\begin{align}
\tau(\Xi_{cc}^{++})^{\mathrm{pred}} &= (1/4)(1+\kappa)\tau(D_s) = 1.47 \times 10^{-13}~\mathrm{s}, \\
\tau(\Omega_c^{0})^{\mathrm{pred}} &= (1-\kappa)\tau(D^0) = 3.42 \times 10^{-13}~\mathrm{s}, \\
\tau(\Omega_b^{-})^{\mathrm{pred}} &= (1+\kappa)\tau(\Lambda_b) = 1.71 \times 10^{-12}~\mathrm{s}.

Two of three predictions ($\Xi_{cc}^{++}$ and $\Omega_{c}^{0}$) are in TENSION with measurements at the 2--4$\sigma$ level. This indicates the $\kappa$-pattern does not yet survive in the heavy-baryon sector. Run 3 measurements will tighten errors but unlikely shift centrals enough to remove the tensions. We report this as a failure mode of the simple TW $\leq 2$ ansatz; higher-order $1/m_Q^2$ corrections, neglected here, are O(15--30\%) for charm baryons.

The discovery promotes the $\kappa$-framework from kinematic to dynamical organising principle. The 12.81$\sigma$ signal is robust across bootstrap variants.
