# Cover letter — Journal of Number Theory

**To:** Editor, *Journal of Number Theory*
**From:** Kévin Rémondière (Oloron-Sainte-Marie, France)
**Date:** 2026-05-20
**Subject:** Submission of *A Center–Rank inequality between the 2-rank of the class group of K_ASP(N) and the 2-adic valuation of N, with a Dirichlet companion*

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit the enclosed paper for consideration in the *Journal of Number Theory*.

The paper proves two inequalities relating arithmetic invariants of imaginary quadratic fields to a positive integer $N$:

- **Theorem CR.** $\mathrm{rk}_2(\mathrm{Cl}(K_{\mathrm{ASP}}(N))) \ge v_2(N)$, conditional on four explicitly named gaps G1–G4. The argument is a three-step combination of (i) the 2-part of the 't Hooft electric flux group on $\mathbb{T}^4$, (ii) the genus theory of Gauss, and (iii) Wilson loop $N$-ality compatibility.

- **Theorem CR'.** $v_2(B_{1,\chi_K}) \ge v_2(N) - v_2(w_K/2)$, *unconditional*, with a three-line proof using only Dirichlet's analytic class-number formula. This inequality is verified empirically over all $153$ fundamental discriminants $D \in [-500, -3]$ by PARI/GP, with $153/153$ PASS.

The paper is short (5–8 pp) and self-contained. It introduces no new arXiv identifier; the cited works are Gauss (Disquisitiones), Dirichlet (Crelle 1839, modern Davenport), Cox (1989), and 't Hooft (Nucl. Phys. B 1979). The Faltings-sweep computation cited in Conjecture 8.1 (saturating regime) is treated as an internal corpus reference (companion note in preparation).

The motivation of the result is the Anchor Selection Principle (ASP) framework for arithmetic-gauge correspondences in SU($N$) Yang–Mills theory. The ASP is treated here as a black box (an input from a wider programme; cf. the companion survey paper, in preparation for Bull. AMS); the present paper is concerned only with the comparison between $v_2(N)$ and $\mathrm{rk}_2(K_{\mathrm{ASP}}(N))$, which is purely arithmetic.

I would be grateful if you could consider this paper for publication.
