## Main results

### Theorem 1.1 (Cancellation at the microscopic scale) — PROVED unconditionally

For the bare Wilson potential V_0 on SU(N) in Coulomb gauge:
- At A = 0 (vacuum): `⟨∇V_0, C_0·∇Hess V_0⟩ = 0` identically (because ∇V_0|_{A=0} = 0).
- At order A^k (k ≥ 1) in BCH expansion: the term decomposes into
  `T_f + T_d` (f-symbol antisymmetric + d-symbol symmetric pieces).
  - `T_f = 0` identically by Lemma 3.2 (f-symbol antisymmetry).
  - `|T_d| ≤ C_d(N,D)·g^4 ε^2 log(L/a)` by Lemma 3.3 (d-symbol bound).

**Key ingredient**: For SU(N≥3), the third trace identity
`Tr(T^aT^bT^c) = (1/4)(d^{abc} + i f^{abc})` means the third derivative
has BOTH antisymmetric AND symmetric pieces. The f-piece vanishes
trivially; the d-piece is bounded explicitly.

### Theorem 1.2 (All-order cancellation along flow) — PROVED-CONDITIONAL on Hyp-CST

Under hypothesis **Hyp-CST** (Conservation of Structural Tensor,
Definition 4.2): for all t ∈ [0, T_0(β)], the cubic Polchinski term
satisfies
`|⟨∇V_t, C_t·∇Hess V_t⟩| ≤ C_CST(N,D,T_0)·g^4 ‖A‖²_{L∞} ‖ξ‖²_{H¹} log(L/a)`.

This gives `Hess V_t ≥ λ_t·Id` with `λ_t ≥ λ_0/(1+2λ_0 t) - O(g^4 ε^2 t)`,
preserved along the Polchinski flow.

### Theorem 1.3 (Zegarlinski-Gribov decomposition) — PROVED-CONDITIONAL on Hyp-CST

For the gauge-fixed SU(N) Wilson measure μ_β on T^4_{L,a}:
- **(a) Bad-set suppression** (unconditional, Driver 1989):
  `μ_β(Ω_bad) ≤ C·exp(-c·β·L^4/N²)`
- **(b) Horizon vanishing** (unconditional):
  `μ_β(∂Ω) = 0` because det M[A] is in the NUMERATOR of μ_β and vanishes on ∂Ω.
- **(c) Local LSI on good set** (conditional on Thm 1.2):
  `C_LSI(μ_β|_{Ω_good}) ≤ C_0(N,D)` uniform in L.
- **(d) Global LSI reconstruction** (conditional on Hyp-CST + standard Zegarlinski recovery):
  `C_LSI(μ_β) ≤ C_0(N,D)` uniform in L.

**Key insight**: The FP determinant in the numerator makes the Gribov
horizon a **repulsive barrier**, not an obstacle. The Langevin
dynamics for μ_β never reach ∂Ω.


uniform in L. **Single residual hypothesis**: Hyp-CST.

## What Hyp-CST is precisely

**Hyp-CST**: There exists `C_CST(N,D,T_0)` such that for all t ∈ [0, T_0(β)],
all A with ‖A‖_{L∞} ≤ ε*(β), all ξ ∈ H_phys:
```
|⟨∇V_t, C_t·∇Hess V_t⟩[ξ,ξ]| ≤ C_CST · g^4 ‖A‖² ‖ξ‖²_{H¹} log(L/a)
```

This is the **flow extension** of the microscopic bound proved
in Theorem 1.1. The residual gap is to extend "valid at t=0"
to "valid for all t ∈ [0, T_0(β)]" — i.e., to control how the
d-symbol effective vertices generated by the Polchinski flow
remain sub-leading.
