Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit for your consideration the short note

> **"An obstruction to the uniform Poincaré route for the Yang–Mills
> mass gap"**

for publication in the *Journal of Functional Analysis*. The note is
3 pages long and reports a *negative* result on a specific functional-
analytic route to the 4D Yang–Mills mass gap.

**Honest framing (no Clay claim).** I am *not* claiming a proof of
the Yang–Mills mass gap. The note is an honest *negative* result on
one particular reduction strategy, in the spirit of classical
negative results in mathematical physics — as a precise map of a
remaining obstacle.

**Structural content.**
(§1) *Conditional theorem.* Under one explicit hypothesis (★) — that
the Poincaré constant of the Langevin generator of the Wilson measure
remains bounded below uniformly as the lattice spacing `a → 0` along
the asymptotic-freedom curve — existing building blocks (Bakry–Émery
at strong coupling, Shen–Zhu–Zhu arXiv:2204.12737; BPHZ stability,
Hairer–Steele arXiv:2301.10081; area-law results,
Cao–Nissim–Sheffield arXiv:2505.16585 & 2509.04688; 3D YM–Higgs
uniqueness, Chevyrev–Shen arXiv:2503.03060; 4D `SU(2)` YM–Higgs
scaling limit, Chatterjee arXiv:2401.10507) assemble into a continuum
mass-gap statement modulo the explicit 4D-state-space caveat.
(§2) *Disambiguation* into three sub-questions: (SubQ1) static ≡
dynamic Poincaré by Bakry–Émery 1985, *not* a source of extra
spread; (SubQ2) Holley–Stroock applies formally but its upper bound
*destroys* uniformity along asymptotic-freedom; (SubQ3) four bypass
routes (Otto–Villani LSI, Eberle reflection couplings, 't Hooft
twist on `T⁴`, partial Coulomb gauge) — three are closed,
the 't Hooft twist remains open.
(§3) *Empirical verdict.* A multi-perspective adversarial analysis
(nine independent DeepSeek–V4–Pro workers + Opus–4.7 audit, full
protocol logged) yields `P((★) true) ∈ [0.05, 0.18]`, median
`0.10–0.12`, *strictly below* the pre-registered 30% pivot.
(§4) *"No known route" ≠ "route closed".* The Holley–Stroock bound
is variationally destructive, not a proof of failure; the 't Hooft
twist sector remains structurally open; the Balaban CMP **95**–**99**,
**109**, **122** series and the fixed-`a` Chatterjee programme are
not addressed here.

**Anti-fabrication.** Two A52-pattern misattributions caught and

unrelated, propagated fabrication; we cite Balaban's CMP series

**166** (arXiv:1305.1233) disambiguated. All 17 references verified
