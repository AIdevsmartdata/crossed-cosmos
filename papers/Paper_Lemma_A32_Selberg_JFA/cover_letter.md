Dear Editor,

I am submitting the manuscript *Per-character spectral decomposition of the Selberg pretrace formula on Bianchi 3-orbifolds via genus-character action* for consideration in the *Journal of Functional Analysis*.

The paper isolates and proves a single technical lemma about the Selberg pretrace formula on the Bianchi 3-orbifold $Y_K = \mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathcal{O}_K)\backslash\mathbb{H}^3$, for $K$ an imaginary quadratic field with arbitrary 2-rank of the class group. The main theorem states that $L^2(Y_K)$ admits a canonical orthogonal decomposition along the Pontryagin dual of the genus character group $g(K) = \mathrm{Cl}(K)/\mathrm{Cl}(K)^2$, that the heat semigroup $e^{-t\Delta}$ block-diagonalises, that the pretrace identity factorises into one absolutely convergent identity per character, and that the cuspidal–continuous splitting is preserved per character.

The decomposition is implicit in Sarnak's 1983 treatment for $h_K\in\{1,2\}$ and in the Eisenstein-series analysis of Elstrodt–Grunewald–Mennicke (Springer SMM 1998), but to my knowledge has not been written down as a single self-contained lemma with full proof and a uniform statement for arbitrary 2-rank. The paper is short (10 pages, amsart format) and the proof reduces entirely to classical published material (Sarnak 1983, EGM 1998, Cox 1989, Dirichlet 1839 via Davenport 2000, Bunke–Olbrich 1995, Gangolli–Warner 1980), with explicit hypothesis-by-hypothesis accounting.

Three computational falsifiables in PARI/GP at $D\in\{-15,-84,-420\}$, covering 2-ranks $\{1,2,3\}$, are stated for numerical verification at an aggregate compute budget of approximately \$95.

This work is **not** a constructive existence claim for Yang–Mills quantum field theory in 3+1 dimensions and makes **no** Clay-Millennium-grade assertion; a tentative consequence for a closed-form mass-gap formula is explicitly deferred to future work with three named blocking sub-issues. The paper aims to contribute a focused, rigorous, well-attributed lemma in spectral theory on Bianchi orbifolds.

I am an independent researcher with no institutional affiliation; the work is part of the open-access *crossed-cosmos* project (concept DOI [10.5281/zenodo.19686398](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19686398)).
