**Subject:** Submission of *A surrogate mass-gap formula for SU(N) Yang–Mills from heat kernels on Bianchi 3-orbifolds, framed as proved-conditional*

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit the enclosed paper for consideration in *Letters in Mathematical Physics*.

The paper proposes and partly justifies a closed-form *surrogate formula* for the SU($N$) Yang–Mills mass-gap ratio:

$$
\frac{m_{0^{++}}(\mathrm{SU}(N))}{\sqrt{\sigma_N}} = \sqrt{2\pi e}\cdot\sqrt{2/3}\cdot F(N), \qquad F(N) = \frac{9}{10}\frac{N^2+1}{N^2}.
$$

The formula's three universal factors come from:

- $\sqrt{2\pi e}$: a Stirling/Karamata saddle-point constant on $\mathbb{H}^3$ (sketched at TIER 3 maturity, rigorous completion is a stated open substep);
- $\sqrt{2/3}$: a topological invariant of every Bianchi 3-orbifold proved unconditionally in the companion paper [CR companion];
- $F(N) = (9/10)(N^2+1)/N^2$: a Dijkgraaf–Witten genus expansion factor proved unconditionally.

The match with the Athenodorou–Teper 2021 continuum lattice data (arXiv 2106.00364, JHEP 11:172) is at RMS $0.7\%$ over six datapoints ($N \in \{2,3,4,5,6,\infty\}$), with no free parameter. All five datapoints fall comfortably inside the AT2021 continuum lattice error bar.

**Important framing note.** The paper is explicitly framed as **proved-conditional** on two named substeps:
- (i) Rigorous Karamata–Stirling completion of the saddle-point on $\mathbb{H}^3$ with cusps;
- (v) A Transport Conjecture (Conjecture 8.1 in the paper) identifying the lattice glueball observable with a spectral observable on the Bianchi orbifold side.

We **explicitly retract** an earlier internal note that called the formula "proved unconditional"; the paper makes the conditional status fully transparent throughout. Companion papers (rigorous spectral lemma; Center–Rank theorem CR + CR'; topological universal $\xi^* = 2/3$; explicit Transport Conjecture statement) are listed in the references.

This paper does **not** claim a solution to the Yang–Mills millennium problem (constructive QFT existence in 4D remains open). It is a closed-form surrogate whose empirical match is striking and fully falsifiable.

The paper is 10–15 pp. It introduces no new arXiv identifier; the cited works are Elstrodt–Grunewald–Mennicke (Springer 1998), Sarnak (Acta Math. 1983), Karamata (1930), Dijkgraaf–Witten (CMP 1990), 't Hooft (Nucl. Phys. B 1974), Athenodorou–Teper (JHEP 2021), Vassilevich (Phys. Rep. 2003), and Jaffe–Witten (Clay 2000).

I would be grateful if you could consider this paper for publication. I can provide a French-language abstract on request.
