Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit for your consideration the manuscript

> **"A one-parameter Sp(2N) glueball mass formula from lattice data"**

for publication in *Physical Review D* as a short paper (4 pp, revtex4-2
reprint style). PRD does not require an external endorser; the submission
is intended to go directly through the APS Editorial Office.

**Result.** Using the continuum-extrapolated lattice glueball spectra
of Bennett *et al.* [Phys. Rev. D **103**, 054509 (2021);
arXiv:2010.15781] for the symplectic sequence and Athenodorou & Teper
[JHEP **12** (2021) 082; arXiv:2106.00364] for the unitary sequence,
I show that the simplest one-parameter ansatz monotone in `1/N`,
`F_Sp(N) ≡ m_{0++}(N)/m_{0++}(3) = (1 + c/N)/(1 + c/3)`, fits the
four `Sp(2N)` continuum points `N ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4}` with
`c = 0.203 ± 0.056` and `χ²/d.o.f. = 0.52/3`. The parallel `SU(N)`
ansatz with `1/N` forbidden by planarity,
`F_SU(N) = (1 + c'/N²)/(1 + c'/9)`, fits the eight points
`N ∈ {2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12}` with `c' = 0.955 ± 0.050` and
`χ²/d.o.f. = 7.15/7`. Both predict the lattice `N → ∞` limit at
`~0.3σ` (`Sp`: 3.21(9) predicted vs. 3.241(88) lattice; `SU`:
3.078(25) vs. 3.072(14)).

**Point of the paper.** The contrast between a *linear* large-`N`
correction in the symplectic sequence and a *quadratic* one in the
unitary sequence is well known structurally (planarity forbids odd
`1/N` powers in `SU(N)` colour singlets; `Sp(2N)` admits both), but
to my knowledge has not been *extracted directly from the lattice
data* as a single one-parameter test with `χ²` reported. The note
performs that test and reports an acceptable fit for both families.

**Honest qualifier.** This is an empirical observation, not a
theorem. The 28% relative uncertainty on `c = 0.203(56)` reflects
that the available `Sp(2N)` continuum points stop at `N = 4`. The
paper closes with explicit predicted values of `m_{0++}/√σ` at
`N ∈ {5, 6, 7, 8}` that would *falsify* the linear ansatz at `>2σ`
— a clean, decidable lattice-falsification programme.

**Anti-fabrication.** Two citation misattributions disambiguated in
the body (§II): (i) "Bennett–Holligan–Hill 2024 PRD **109**" does not
exist; the canonical reference is the eight-author Bennett *et al.*
2021 (arXiv:2010.15781); (ii) the misquoted "Athenodorou–Teper
arXiv:2011.07257" → correct identifier arXiv:2106.00364. All arXiv
IDs in `refs.bib` verified live against the arXiv API. The
