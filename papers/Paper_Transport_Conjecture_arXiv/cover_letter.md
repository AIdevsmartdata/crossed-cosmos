## Summary

This preprint isolates, as a *single explicit open conjecture*, the identification between (a) the lattice SU($N$) Yang–Mills scalar glueball mass and (b) a spectral observable on a Bianchi 3-orbifold $Y_K = \mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathcal{O}_K)\backslash\mathbb{H}^3$ attached to a privileged anchor $K = K_{\mathrm{ASP}}(N)$.

The conjecture is the principal *open substep* in a surrogate mass-gap formula proposed in a companion paper [Remondière 2026 RouteB]. The empirical match between conjecture and lattice data (Athenodorou–Teper 2021) is at RMS $0.7\%$ over six datapoints, but the conjecture is **explicitly stated as open** and **not** claimed to be proved.

The preprint:

- Gives the explicit formal statement (Conjecture 1.1);
- Lists four candidate proof strategies (CCHS 4D extension; Cao–Sheffield random surfaces; Wightman positivity W0; Cluster decomposition W5), with honest status assessments;
- Lists five falsifiable lattice-Yang–Mills predictions (F-T-1 through F-T-5);
- Provides an honest open-problem assessment in §4.

No new arXiv identifier is introduced. Companion papers cited as [Remondière 2026 Selberg / CenterRank / CR / RouteB] are listed as the corpus dependencies.
