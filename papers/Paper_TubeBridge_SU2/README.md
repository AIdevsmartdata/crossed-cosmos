## What version 1.2 adds (Changes since v1.1)
- **Section "The cyclic centre Z_N"** of the note (Section 6) and the folder `lean/CentreZN/`. For a cyclic centre of order N, with ζ = e^{2πi/N}, the Fourier transforms Ẑ_q = Σ_k ζ^{qk} Z_k over the centre twists of the Wilson partition function (the electric-flux projectors of Kanazawa, up to normalisation) satisfy |Ẑ_q| ≤ ρ^{|P|} Ẑ_0 with ρ = 1 − e^{−2κ} for every N and q ≢ 0 (Lean); the case N = 2 of the published statements `column_flip_bound` and `twist_ratio_bound` is *derived* from it in Lean. **Lean (16 declarations, namespace `CentreZN`, imports `Mathlib` only):** the abstract open- and closed-surface bounds with an arbitrary constant ρ satisfying a stated hypothesis (H_ρ), the constant 1 − e^{−2κ}, the case N = 2, the sharpness example, the Wilson-type versions on (torus) × (fibre), the torus charge lemmas. **Paper only:** the improvement ρ ≤ tanh κ for all N (three lines), the reduction of the optimal constant to the circle |t| = κ, its closed forms for N = 3 and 4, the corollary for SU(N) on the periodic 4D lattice (hypotheses checked one by one) and the composition with Kanazawa's Theorem 2. **Conjecture:** the closed form of the optimal constant for N ≥ 5. **Not claimed:** anything about the mass gap, uniformity in β or in the section, the neutral sector.
- Classical context: the quantity is the Z_N Fourier transform over the centre of 't Hooft's twisted partition functions; the tool is the centre-multiplication change of variables (Tomboulis–Yaffe 1985, App. I.C and p. 333; Kovács–Tomboulis 2002; Kanazawa 2008). We did not find, in the texts we read, an explicit bound at every coupling; this is not a claim of absence (`ANTERIORITE_v1_2.md`).
- Rebuild of the 16 declarations by a model that did not write them (`lean/CentreZN/CERTIFICAT_ZN_REJEU.md`), reviews by DeepSeek V4 Pro and GPT-6 Astra (`RELECTURES_ZN.md`), concordance extended (Part II of `CONCORDANCE_TEXTE_LEAN.md`).

## What the note says, and does not say (versions 1.0 and 1.1; unchanged)
- Abstract bound `|⟨W⟩| ≤ tanh(κ)^|A|` and its sharpness (Lean). Elementary area law `tanh(β|F|)^area` on (rectangular plate) × (finite fibre F) for Wilson-type actions with a central involution (Lean).
- Torus: plaquette signs multiply to 1 and every even assignment is realised (Lean); twist-ratio bound `|Z₀ − Z₁| ≤ tanh(κ)^N (Z₀ + Z₁)` (Lean).
- **Head theorem, on OpenAI's objects** (Lean, compiled inside their project): for every `l ≥ 2` and `β ≥ 0`, `|Z₀ − Z₁| ≤ tanh(β l²)^(l²) (Z₀ + Z₁)` for their periodic lattice, their Wilson energy and their SU(2), where `Z₁` has the sign of the trace term of the plaquettes of the plane of axes 0,1 at one base point (all positions along axes 2,3) reversed (`TubeBridge.openai_su2_twist_ratio`).
- On paper only (checked by AI models only, not formalised): `λ₁/λ₀ ≤ tanh(β L²)^l` for the odd and even centre-parity sectors of a tube transfer matrix (Section 5).
- **Not claimed**: any mass gap; anything uniform in the transverse section (the bound tends to 1 as `l` or `|F|` grows at fixed β); anything in the weak-coupling regime, in infinite volume or in the continuum; any gap in the neutral sector; the sharpness of the constant for SU(2). The argument uses only a central involution and `|χ| ≤ 1`, so it holds verbatim for U(1) and Z₂ gauge theories.
- Written by AI models and read by other AI models (roles in the note); not refereed; no human has checked the proofs.
