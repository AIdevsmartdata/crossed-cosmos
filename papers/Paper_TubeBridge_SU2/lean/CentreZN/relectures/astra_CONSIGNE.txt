You are asked to derive, INDEPENDENTLY and with full proofs, the following results; nothing is given to you to check, you must produce the derivation yourself and say honestly where you fail. You have no tools; reason in plain text. Answer in English, structured as Part 1, Part 2, Part 3, ending with the line "END".

SETTING. Let N >= 1, zeta = exp(2 pi i/N) (primitive N-th root of unity), Z_N = Z/NZ. For t in C and q in Z_N put
f_j(t) = exp(Re(zeta^j t)) (j in Z_N), S(t) = sum_j f_j(t), A_q(t) = sum_j zeta^{qj} f_j(t).

PART 1 (one-plaquette ratio). Prove or refute: for q not = 0 mod N and all t in C, |A_q(t)| <= (1 - exp(-2|t|)) S(t). Then find the best constant rho_{N,q}(kappa) = sup_{|t|<=kappa} |A_q(t)|/S(t); give closed forms where you can prove them (N=2, N=3, N=4) and say what you can and cannot prove for N>=5.

PART 2 (abstract averaging bound). Let (Omega, mu) be a probability space, P a finite set of "plaquettes", T_p : Omega -> C measurable with |T_p| <= kappa, R >= 0 an invariant weight. Suppose a family of measure-preserving maps tau_s with T_p(tau_s w) = zeta^{c_p(s)} T_p(w), R(tau_s w) = R(w), whose charge vectors c(s) = (c_p(s))_p in Z_N^P cover every vector of Z_N^P whose coordinates sum to 0. Let p0 in P, and Z_k = integral R exp(sum_p Re(eps_{k,p} T_p)) dmu with eps_{k,p} = zeta^k if p = p0 and 1 otherwise. Determine the best possible bound of the form |sum_k zeta^{qk} Z_k| <= C * sum_k Z_k in terms of the constant of Part 1, and prove it. State precisely the integrability/measurability assumptions you use. Do the same for the "open surface" variant: an observable W with |W|<=1 and W(tau_s w) = zeta^{q sum_{p in A} c_p(s)} W(w) for a subset A of P, all charge vectors being realised: bound |integral W R exp(sum Re T_p)| in terms of |A|.

PART 3 (lattice gauge theory, the case that matters). Gauge group SU(N), periodic hypercubic lattice (Z/l)^4, l >= 2, Haar measure on every link, Wilson action weight exp(beta sum_{x, mu<nu} Re Tr U_{x,mu nu}/N), U_{x,mu nu} = U(x,mu)U(x+mu,nu)U(x+nu,mu)^{-1}U(x,nu)^{-1}. Twist k in Z_N in the plane (0,1) at the base point (a,b): in the plaquettes (x,(0,1)) with (x0,x1)=(a,b) (all x2,x3), Re Tr U is replaced by Re(zeta^k Tr U). Z_k = integral of the twisted weight. Prove, for q not = 0 mod N, a bound of the form |sum_k zeta^{qk} Z_k| <= C(beta,l,N,q) sum_k Z_k with C of the form (constant of Part 1 at kappa = beta*l^2)^(l^2), VERIFYING EVERY HYPOTHESIS you need by hand: which transformations of the link variables you use, why they preserve the Haar measure, how the weight and the twisted plaquettes transform, why all charge vectors with sum 0 are realised (do the case l = 2 explicitly), and why all the other plaquettes (those not in the plane (0,1), and those in it at other base points) are invariant. If the statement is false as stated, say so and give the correct one. Finally compare with the known flux-projector formalism (Tomboulis-Yaffe / Kanazawa): say how the quantity sum_k zeta^{qk} Z_k relates to it, up to normalisation, from your own memory, marking anything you are not sure about [MEMORY, UNVERIFIED].
