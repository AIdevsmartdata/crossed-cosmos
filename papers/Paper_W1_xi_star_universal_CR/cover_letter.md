# Cover letter — Comptes Rendus de l'Académie des Sciences, Mathématique

**To:** Editor, Comptes Rendus Mathématique
**From:** Kévin Rémondière (Oloron-Sainte-Marie, France)

**Subject:** Submission of *A topological invariant of Bianchi 3-orbifolds from the identity term of the Selberg pretrace formula*

Dear Editor,

I am pleased to submit the enclosed note for consideration in *Comptes Rendus Mathématique*. The note isolates, from the identity term of the Selberg pretrace formula on a Bianchi 3-orbifold $Y_K = \mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathcal{O}_K)\backslash \mathbb{H}^3$ attached to an imaginary quadratic field $K$, a real number $\xi^*(K) = 1/(1+|\text{residue ratio}|)$, and proves that:

(i) the residue ratio at the only two relevant poles $s\in\{1/2, 3/2\}$ equals exactly $-\Gamma(3/2)/\Gamma(1/2) = -1/2$, *independently of $K$*; and

(ii) the hyperbolic, elliptic, parabolic and Eisenstein contributions to the Selberg pretrace formula are entire at both $s=1/2$ and $s=3/2$, hence do not modify the ratio.

The consequence is that $\xi^*(K) = 2/3$ is a *topological invariant* of every Bianchi 3-orbifold — universal across class numbers, discriminants and 2-ranks.

The proof is short (3-5 pp), self-contained, and based on standard tools of Elstrodt–Grunewald–Mennicke (Springer 1998), Bunke–Olbrich (Akademie 1995) and Vassilevich's Seeley–DeWitt summary (Phys. Rep. 2003). It introduces no new arXiv identifier. The numerical verification at 100 digits across 8 discriminants $D\in\{-3,-7,-11,-15,-19,-43,-67,-163\}$ (Heegner and $\mathrm{rk}_2\geq 1$) is provided as evidence of the universality, alongside one explicit falsifiable F-W1-CROSS-D.

The note's scope is deliberately narrow: it asserts only the universality and topological nature of $\xi^*(K)$ as a spectral residue ratio. No physical interpretation (in particular no Koide-style correspondence) is claimed, and a recently observed numerical coincidence between $\xi^*$ and the Koide ratio of charged-lepton masses (Belle II 2024) is explicitly discussed as a coincidence of rational numbers, not a structural correspondence.

I would be grateful if you could consider this short note for publication. I am happy to provide a French-language version on request (my native language).
